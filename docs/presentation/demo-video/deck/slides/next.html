What we learned

It never touches who or when. Its JSON output has no speaker or time fields; both are copied from diarization.

Its “repair” does not help. +0.9 points of cpWER on 63 test conversations, so the ASR transcript stays the record.

Errors cascade. Keywords really said: 99% from the true transcript, 60% from real ASR.

Where we go next

Who-spoke-when. About 20 of the 49.5 points, mostly speaker confusion. Next: fine-tune the speaker embeddings on Hindi conversations.

Action items. Empty in all 252 reports from the 7B model. Next: a larger LLM, one setting away.

Separation. Conv-TasNet was trained on English mixtures (Libri2Mix). Next: Indian voices and noise.
